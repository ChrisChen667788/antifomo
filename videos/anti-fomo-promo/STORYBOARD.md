# Anti-FOMO Overview Storyboard

This video tells solution architects that collected signals can become a more deliberate next customer conversation.

| Frame | Time | User value | Historical product source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | 0–3 s | Too much saved. Too little decided. | Home signal dashboard |
| 02 | 3–6 s | Build a view you can explain. | Inbox research workspace |
| 03 | 6–9 s | See the change. Keep the context. | Research compare workspace |
| 04 | 9–12 s | Meet the customer with context. | Knowledge commercial hub |
| 05 | 12–15 s | Make your next move deliberate. | Focus session workspace |
