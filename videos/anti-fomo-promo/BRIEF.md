# Anti-FOMO Promo Brief

Audience: solution architects, consultants, pre-sales and open-source builders.

Pain: information is collected in many places, but source freshness, evidence gaps and the next action remain unclear.

Promise: Anti-FOMO turns signals into a reviewable evidence journey from intake to architecture and action.

Proof: real local UI captures, source-health states, claim/source revision, compare, HOLD and human review receipts. Conceptual assets must be labeled as demo.

CTA: run Quick Start, inspect the evidence boundary, then contribute an adapter, benchmark, diagram or review workflow.
